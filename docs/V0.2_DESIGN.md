# InsightAI v0.2 Design Blueprint

> **Theme:** AI Business Analyst & Executive Decision Workspace

---

# Purpose

Version 0.2 transforms InsightAI from an AI reporting tool into an AI-powered Decision Intelligence Platform.

Instead of producing reports, InsightAI begins guiding users through a structured business reasoning process similar to how experienced consultants and business analysts work.

---

# Design Goals

Version 0.2 should achieve four objectives:

- Make AI conversations business-focused.
- Deliver executive-ready insights.
- Recommend actions, not just observations.
- Create a premium consulting experience.

---

# Executive Decision Workspace

The AI Workspace becomes the heart of InsightAI.

Instead of a single "Generate Report" button, users enter a workspace where they can explore their business from different perspectives.

Primary capabilities include:

- Executive Briefing
- KPI Interpretation
- Business Performance Analysis
- Root Cause Analysis
- Risk Assessment
- Opportunity Discovery
- Strategic Recommendations
- Scenario Exploration
- Executive Questions & Answers

# Success Criteria

Version 0.2 is successful when users no longer describe InsightAI as:

> "A tool that generates AI reports."

Instead, they say:

> "InsightAI helps me understand my business and make better decisions."

That is the transformation v0.2 is designed to achieve.
