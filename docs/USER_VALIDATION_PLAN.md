# User Validation Questions

## 1. Current Workflow

- How do you currently analyze business data and generate insights?
- What tools do you typically use?
- How much manual effort goes into preparing analysis and recommendations?

---

## 2. Pain Points

- What is the most time-consuming part of your analytics workflow?
- What parts of business analysis still require too much manual work?
- Where do current BI/AI tools fall short?

---

## 3. InsightAI Experience

After testing InsightAI:

- What part of the experience was most valuable?
- What part was unclear or difficult to use?
- Did the generated insights feel relevant to business decisions?
- Would you trust these recommendations in a professional setting?

---

## 4. Missing Capabilities

- What would make this tool significantly more useful for your work?
- What questions would you want InsightAI to answer?
- What data sources would you expect it to connect with?

---

## 5. Adoption

- Could you see yourself using something like this at work?
- In what situations would you use it?
- What would prevent adoption?
