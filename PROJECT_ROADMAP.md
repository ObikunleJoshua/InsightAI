# InsightAI Product Roadmap

## Vision

InsightAI is an AI Decision Intelligence Platform designed to help business leaders, consultants, and transformation teams make better decisions by combining Business Intelligence, AI reasoning, and structured business knowledge.

InsightAI is not just an analytics tool.

It is designed to act as an AI Business Analyst and Decision Partner.

# Current Development Focus

# v0.3 — AI Business Analyst

Status: Next Sprint 🚀

## Objective

Transform InsightAI from a report generation platform into an interactive AI business analyst.

Users should be able to ask business questions and receive decision-focused answers.

---

## Key Capabilities

### Business Question Interface

Allow users to ask:

Examples:

- Why did profitability decline?
- Which products should we focus on?
- What are the biggest risks?
- Which customers create the most value?
- Where should management invest?

---

### AI Reasoning Layer

The AI should consider:

- Dataset context
- Decision context
- Business objectives
- User persona
- Analysis objective

before generating recommendations.

---

### Executive Conversation Experience

Move from:

Upload → Report

towards:

Upload → Ask → Analyze → Recommend → Decide

# Product Principles

Every feature must answer:

"Would a consultant use this to advise a client?"

If the answer is no, defer it.

---

## InsightAI should:

✅ Help users understand business problems

✅ Provide explainable recommendations

✅ Combine data with business reasoning

✅ Support decision-making

---

## InsightAI should not:

❌ Become another generic chatbot

❌ Generate meaningless dashboards

❌ Replace business thinking with AI output

❌ Focus only on technical analytics
